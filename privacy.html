Privacy Policy for CassavaScan

Effective date: November 2026

1. Introduction

CassavaScan ("we", "our", "the app") is an Android app that checks cassava leaves for common diseases. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app uses and how.

2. Information We Collect

The app does not ask for your name, phone number, email address, or location, and it does not collect any of them. Photos you take or choose are analysed on your phone. The app does not upload them to any server.

3. How We Use Information

Photos are used only to show a disease result and advice for the leaf in that photo.

4. Data Storage

Your scan history is stored on your phone only. Uninstalling the app removes it.

5. Third-Party Services

The app does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking services, and it does not share data with third parties. Links in the About screen open in your web browser, and those websites have their own privacy policies.

6. Permissions

The app asks for permission to use your camera, so you can photograph a leaf. It asks for access to your photos only when you choose a picture from your gallery. It does not use these permissions for any other purpose.

7. Security

Because scanning happens on your device, your photos and history are protected by your phone's own security settings. We recommend setting a screen lock on your phone.

8. Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page, and the effective date will be updated.
