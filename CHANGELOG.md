# Changelog

All notable changes to CassavaScan are listed here, newest first.

## v1.0.0

First test release.

- Android app named CassavaScan, based on the open-source CassavaCare project (MIT licence)
- Image model trained with Google Teachable Machine, packaged as TensorFlow Lite
- Six classes: Bacterial Blight, Brown Streak, Green Mottle, Mosaic, Healthy, and Not a Cassava Leaf
- Camera and gallery scanning, scan history, and disease information screens
- New look: green and amber colour scheme, light and dark mode, redesigned home, scan, history, and information screens
- Random farming tips on the home screen
- Works offline once installed
- Field testing not yet completed
